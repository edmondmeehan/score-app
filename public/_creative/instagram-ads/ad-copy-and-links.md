## Campaign settings (test)
- Objective: Leads → conversion location Website, optimize for the waitlist signup event (Lead). If the pixel event isn't verified yet, start with Traffic → landing page views.
- Budget: $50/day, campaign budget (Advantage), 14 days.
- Location: New York City + 10 mi (people living in or recently in). Age 25–54. All genders.
- Placements: Instagram Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore (Advantage+ placements off; Instagram only).
- Special ad category: none expected; if Meta asks because of "money/credit" wording, follow its prompt.
- Comments: reply to every comment in the first hour; hide spam.
- Kill rule after 3–4 days: pause any ad with cost per signup 2× the average; move budget to the best one.

## Rules
- No "verified credit" or "we check your score" claims while bands are self-reported.
- No fake member quotes or testimonials.
- Music: add a trending licensed track inside Instagram when publishing the Reels (videos are silent on purpose).
